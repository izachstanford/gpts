# Privacy Policy for Whetstones of Wisdom

**Effective Date:** \[Insert Date\]

Whetstones of Wisdom is a personal AI assistant built by Zach Stanford
to surface and organize quotes from books and other sources.

## Data Collection

-   The app fetches quotes from a connected Google Sheet that Zach
    maintains.\
-   Only Zach's account can add new quotes to the sheet.\
-   No personal or user-identifiable information is collected, stored,
    or shared.

## Data Usage

-   Queries are processed to search for relevant quotes within the quote
    database.\
-   If Zach chooses to append a quote, it is stored only in his private
    Google Sheet.\
-   No other data is stored or retained by the app.

## Data Sharing

-   No data is sold, shared, or transferred to third parties.\
-   Access to append quotes is limited to Zach's authenticated Google
    account.
